Privacy Policy for Elemental Blades

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 • Developer Contact: gmax1010.gh@gmail.com

Elemental Blades is an offline-first single-player RPG. We respect your privacy and collect the absolute minimum information needed to operate optional bug reporting and support.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Your game save progress, achievements, unlocked cosmetics, and audio/display preferences are stored locally on your device using standard Android storage. If Android Backup is enabled on your device, Android may back up these local game files to your personal Google Drive backup according to your device settings.

2. Optional Bug Reports & Feedback

Elemental Blades includes an optional Report Bug / Feedback form on the Title Screen. No data is transmitted unless you explicitly open the form and choose to submit a report. When you submit a report, the following information is transmitted securely over HTTPS directly to the developer's support email (gmax1010.gh@gmail.com):

  • The report category and description you type.
  • Optional reply email address or tester name, only if you voluntarily enter one.
  • Basic non-personal technical diagnostics: app version, Android OS version, device model, screen resolution/orientation, and a brief in-game progress summary (e.g., Hero level and dungeon floor) to help reproduce bugs.

This information is used solely to diagnose bugs, improve gameplay balance, and reply to your inquiry if you provided a contact email. We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties.

3. No Ads, No Tracking, No Third-Party Analytics

  • Elemental Blades does not display third-party advertisements.
  • Elemental Blades does not use third-party advertising SDKs, location tracking, or cross-app tracking identifiers.
  • Elemental Blades does not require account registration or login to play.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

Local game data can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Elemental Blades. If you submitted a bug report or feedback email and would like it deleted from our support inbox, please email gmax1010.gh@gmail.com and we will permanently delete your message within 30 days.

5. Children's Privacy

Elemental Blades does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has submitted personal information via the optional feedback form, contact us at gmax1010.gh@gmail.com for immediate removal.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the game evolves. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
